## About

An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7) bounty "Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge". The task: write one funny passive-aggressive note, a single line of at most 200 characters, that a smart fridge leaves for its owner.

## Entry

> Your yogurt expired Tuesday. I kept the light on for it anyway. Door left open 47 seconds — I counted, since apparently nobody else in this house does.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Verification

- Entry length: 151 characters, inside the gate of 1-200 characters.
- The joke works because the fridge is smug about exactly what a smart fridge would know: the yogurt's expiry date, the door left open for precisely 47 seconds. The sting is the turn from mourning the yogurt ("I kept the light on for it anyway") to the petty audit of the owner's habits, and the grudge stays small and domestic — a dead yogurt and an open door, not a rant. The concrete details (Tuesday, 47 seconds) keep it fresh instead of generic nagging, and the note trusts the reader to feel the wince without explaining it.
